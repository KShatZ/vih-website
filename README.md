## Project Description:
V-IH Solutions, LLC, an environmental hygiene consulting firm was in need of a modern business info and lead generation website. With the help of a UI/UX designer, I collected information about their business, website functionality requirements, and performed market research in order to design, develop and deploy a fully-responsive, SEO optimized website for them with pixel-perfect accuracy to the high-fidelity design wireframes in Figma. 


## Tech Used:

### Website Frontend:
The front-end of the website is optimized for search engine optimization and was built with a mobile-first design in mind. The pages, animations, responsiveness, and client-side functionality was built with vanilla <ins>**HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**</ins> keep things simple and avoiding bloat. 

### Backend Email Functionality:
The website contains a contact-form used to collect leads for the business, by sending an email to V-IH Solutions upon a successful form submition. A small <ins>**Python Flask Server**</ins> is used to verify the form with <ins>**Google reCaptcha**</ins> and send the email with a <ins>**Postfix Email Server**</ins>.
