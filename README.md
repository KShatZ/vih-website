## Project Description:
V-IH Solutions, LLC, an environmental hygiene consulting firm, needed a modern business information and lead generation website. Collaborating with a UI/UX designer, I gathered information about their business, identified website functionality requirements, and conducted market research to design, develop, and deploy a fully responsive, SEO-optimized website. The site was implemented with pixel-perfect accuracy based on high-fidelity design wireframes created in Figma.

## Tech Used:

### Website Frontend:
The website's front end was built with a mobile-first design approach and optimized for search engine performance. The pages, animations, responsiveness, and client-side functionality were developed using vanilla <ins>**HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**</ins> to keep the implementation simple and avoid unnecessary bloat.

### Backend Email Functionality:
The website includes a contact form for collecting business leads by sending emails to the company upon successful form submission. A lightweight <ins>**Python Flask server**</ins> was implemented to validate the form using <ins>**Google reCaptcha**</ins> and handle email delivery via a <ins>**Postfix Email Server**</ins>.
